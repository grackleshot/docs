SupportDesk is the support ticketing platform built for SaaS teams where every ticket matters. Unlike generic helpdesks designed for high-volume, transactional support, SupportDesk is purpose-built for engineers, solutions teams, and customer success managers who handle complex, high-stakes cases that require deep context, structured collaboration, and precise SLA control. If your team spends hours — not seconds — on each ticket, SupportDesk is built for you.

## Get up and running

Whether you're migrating from another helpdesk or starting fresh, you can have SupportDesk fully operational in minutes. Follow these steps to go from zero to your first resolved ticket.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for SupportDesk">
    Go to [app.supportdesk.io](https://app.supportdesk.io) and create your account. No credit card required during your free trial.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Choose a workspace name and subdomain (e.g., `acme.supportdesk.io`). This is your team's isolated environment for all tickets, agents, and settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your support email">
    Navigate to **Settings → Channels → Email** and connect your support address (e.g., `support@acme.com`). Incoming emails automatically become tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your first ticket">
    Send a test email to your support address or create a ticket manually from the dashboard. Assign it to yourself and send a reply — you're live.
  </Step>
</Steps>
