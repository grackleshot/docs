SupportDesk integrations let your team bring support context into the tools you already use — and push ticket activity out to the systems that power your product. Instead of jumping between your inbox, a chat app, and a separate ticketing tool, you can centralize every customer conversation, escalation, and resolution in one place. Whether you're routing inbound email to tickets, firing Slack alerts when SLAs are at risk, or building a custom integration with webhooks, SupportDesk connects to the infrastructure your SaaS team depends on.

## Integration Categories

### Communication Channels

Communication channel integrations are the primary way tickets enter SupportDesk. Every message your customers send — whether through email or a web form — is converted into a ticket with a requester, subject, and conversation thread.

- **Email** — Connect your support address (e.g., `support@yourcompany.com`) using email forwarding or MX records. Replies thread back into the same ticket automatically.
- **Web Form** — Embed a customizable support form on your website or help center. Submissions create tickets with structured fields for faster triage.

### Collaboration Tools

Collaboration integrations keep your internal team informed without requiring them to stay logged into SupportDesk all day.

- **Slack** — Route ticket events to specific Slack channels. Get notified when a new ticket arrives, an SLA is about to breach, or a critical escalation is triggered.

### Developer Integrations

Developer integrations are designed for teams that want to extend SupportDesk or connect it to proprietary tooling.

- **Webhooks** — Subscribe to real-time ticket events and receive structured JSON payloads at any HTTPS endpoint you control.
- **REST API** — Create and manage tickets, agents, teams, and custom fields programmatically. Useful for building customer-facing portals, internal dashboards, or automated ticket creation from your product.

## Getting Started

Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** in your SupportDesk workspace to connect any of the integrations above. Each integration has its own configuration page where you can manage credentials, notification preferences, and routing rules.
