SupportDesk is a modern support ticketing platform built specifically for SaaS teams that handle low-volume, high-complexity technical cases. While most helpdesks are optimized for speed and throughput, SupportDesk is optimized for depth — giving your team the context, collaboration tools, and workflow control needed to resolve the kinds of tickets that can't be answered with a canned response. Every feature is designed around the assumption that each ticket deserves your full attention.

## Who SupportDesk is built for

SupportDesk is the right fit if your support team operates more like an engineering function than a call center. It's purpose-built for:

- **SaaS companies with dedicated support engineers** who regularly diagnose technical issues, review logs, and coordinate with product and engineering teams.
- **Solutions engineers and technical account managers** who support enterprise customers through integrations, onboarding, and advanced configurations.
- **Customer success teams** managing a book of accounts where deep relationships and response quality matter more than ticket volume.
- **Early- and growth-stage SaaS teams** that need a serious support workflow without the overhead and noise of enterprise helpdesk platforms.

If your team handles dozens of tickets per week — not thousands — and each one carries real business impact, SupportDesk is designed for you.

## How SupportDesk is different

Generic helpdesks like Zendesk and Freshdesk were built to scale horizontally across massive support organizations. That makes them powerful for high-volume, low-complexity workflows — but bloated and distracting for teams where complexity is the norm. SupportDesk takes a different approach.

## What SupportDesk is NOT

SupportDesk is intentionally scoped. It is **not** designed for:

- **High-volume transactional support** (e.g., e-commerce returns, password resets at scale). If you're handling thousands of tickets per day, you likely need a different tool.
- **Contact center or phone support workflows.** SupportDesk is built around async, written communication — not call queues or live voice channels.
- **Consumer-facing self-service portals** with large public knowledge bases and community forums.

If your team lives in the weeds of every ticket and needs a platform that respects that complexity, SupportDesk is the right choice.
