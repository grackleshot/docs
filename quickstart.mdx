This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a blank slate to a fully operational SupportDesk workspace. By the end, you'll have your team set up, your support email connected, and your first ticket created, assigned, and replied to. No lengthy configuration required — you can refine your workflow as you go.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Go to [app.supportdesk.io](https://app.supportdesk.io) and sign up for a free account. During signup, you'll be prompted to:

    - Choose a **workspace name** (typically your company name, e.g., `Acme Support`).
    - Set a **subdomain** (e.g., `acme.supportdesk.io`) — this becomes your team's unique SupportDesk URL.

    <Note>
      You'll receive a verification email after signup. Click the link in that email before proceeding — some setup steps, including email channel configuration, require a verified account.
    </Note>

    Once verified, you'll land on your workspace dashboard, ready to configure.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your team members">
    Your workspace is only as useful as the people in it. To add your team:

    1. Navigate to **Settings → Agents**.
    2. Click **Invite Agent** and enter each person's email address.
    3. Assign each agent a **role**:
       - **Admin** — full access to settings, billing, and all tickets.
       - **Agent** — can view, respond to, and manage tickets within their assigned queues.
       - **Viewer** — read-only access, useful for stakeholders who need visibility without the ability to act.

    Invited teammates will receive an email prompting them to accept and set up their account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your support email">
    Connecting your email channel turns incoming emails into tickets automatically — no manual entry required.

    1. Go to **Settings → Channels → Email**.
    2. Enter your support email address (e.g., `support@acme.com`).
    3. Choose your connection method:
       - **Email forwarding** — add a forwarding rule in your email provider (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) to forward messages to the address shown in SupportDesk.
       - **Custom MX record** — update your domain's MX records to route mail directly through SupportDesk for tighter integration.

    <Tip>
      Take a few minutes now to set up **custom fields** on your tickets — things like `Product Area`, `Customer Tier`, or `Environment`. Adding these early makes triage, routing, and reporting significantly more powerful from day one.
    </Tip>

    Once connected, send a test email to your support address and confirm it appears as a ticket in your inbox.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first ticket">
    You can create a ticket in one of two ways:

    - **Via email** — send a message to your connected support address. It will appear in your ticket inbox within seconds.
    - **Manually** — click **New Ticket** in the dashboard, fill in the subject, requester, description, and priority, and save.

    Either way, your first ticket is now open and waiting for action.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign and respond">
    With your first ticket open, close the loop:

    1. Click **Assign** and select yourself (or another agent) as the assignee.
    2. Set the **priority** (Low, Normal, High, or Urgent) based on the nature of the issue.
    3. Write a reply in the response editor. You can use rich text, attach files, or add an **internal note** visible only to your team.
    4. Click **Send Reply** — the response is delivered to the requester and the ticket status updates automatically.

    Congratulations — you've completed your first full ticket workflow in SupportDesk.
  </Step>
</Steps>
