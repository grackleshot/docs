SupportDesk offers three plans designed to grow with your support team — from a small team handling a handful of complex tickets to an enterprise operation with advanced SLA requirements and dedicated support. Your billing settings live under **Settings → Billing** and give you a single place to view your current plan, upgrade or downgrade, update your payment method, and access your invoice history. This page explains what's included in each plan and walks you through the most common billing tasks.

## Billing Cycle

SupportDesk bills on either a **monthly** or **annual** cycle:

- **Monthly billing** — You're charged at the start of each billing period. Cancel any time and your subscription remains active until the end of the current period.
- **Annual billing** — You're charged once per year upfront. Annual plans include a **20% discount** compared to the equivalent monthly rate. Annual plans are non-refundable after the billing date but can be upgraded at any time.

You can switch between monthly and annual billing from **Settings → Billing → Billing Cycle**. Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately; switching from annual to monthly takes effect at the end of your current annual term.

## Managing Your Subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing settings">
    Go to **Settings → Billing** in your SupportDesk workspace. You must be an Admin to access billing settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your current plan">
    The top of the Billing page shows your current plan, the number of active agent seats in use, and your next billing date and amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Upgrade or Downgrade">
    Click **Upgrade Plan** to move to a higher tier, or **Downgrade Plan** to move to a lower tier. A summary of the changes and any prorated charges or credits will be shown before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the changes">
    Review the confirmation screen and click **Confirm Change**. Plan upgrades take effect immediately. Plan downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating Your Payment Method

To change the credit card or payment method on file:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Method**.
2. Click **Update Payment Method**.
3. Enter your new card details in the secure payment form (processed by Stripe).
4. Click **Save**. Your new card will be charged on the next billing date.

SupportDesk accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. For Enterprise customers paying by invoice, contact your account manager.

## Invoices

SupportDesk automatically generates an invoice for every successful charge. To access your invoice history:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
2. Find the invoice you need in the list — invoices are sorted by date, most recent first.
3. Click **Download PDF** to save the invoice, or **View** to see a browser version.

Invoices are also sent automatically to your workspace's **billing email address** after each successful charge. To update the billing email, go to **Settings → Billing → Billing Email**.

## Cancellation

To cancel your SupportDesk subscription:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Cancel Subscription** at the bottom of the page.
3. Select a cancellation reason (optional) and confirm.

After cancellation, your workspace remains fully accessible until the end of your current billing period. After that, your workspace enters a **30-day read-only grace period** — you can view and export your ticket data, but you cannot create new tickets or add agents. After 30 days, your workspace and all associated data is permanently deleted.

If you change your mind during the grace period, you can reactivate your subscription from the Billing page to restore full access.

<Note>
  When you upgrade your plan mid-billing-cycle, SupportDesk calculates a **prorated charge** for the remainder of the current period. For example, if you upgrade from Starter to Growth 15 days into a 30-day billing cycle, you'll be charged for half a month of the Growth plan minus the unused half-month credit from your Starter plan. The prorated amount is shown clearly on the confirmation screen before you confirm the upgrade.
</Note>
